Lane assist, end to end

From pixels to steering — with hard limits in between.

Every frame, Wayline OS turns camera pixels into a lane model, plans a path, and asks for exactly as much steering as it needs — never more than an independent safety layer allows.

  1. Perception perception/lanes

    Lane markings are detected and fitted to a curve, giving lateral offset, heading and road curvature — with a confidence score.

  2. Planning controls.py

    A smooth path back to lane center, with curvature feed-forward so the car leans into bends instead of reacting late.

  3. Control control/lateral

    The path becomes a steering-torque request, tuned for comfort and consistency at every speed.

  4. Safety limits control/safety

    Torque and its rate of change are clamped. Touch the brake or turn the wheel and you're in control — instantly, every time.

    Driver monitoring

    Hands off is fine. Eyes off is not.

    Wayline is driver assistance, not self-driving. An infrared camera watches for distraction and drowsiness, and the response escalates step by step — all the way to bringing the car to a controlled stop if you stop responding.

    1. AttentiveEyes on the road — assistance stays engaged
      ok
    2. Visual alertEyes off the road for a few seconds
      step 1
    3. Audible alertChime and spoken prompt
      step 2
    4. Take controlUrgent alert — driver must respond
      step 3
    5. Emergency Stop AssistSlows to a stop in lane, hazards on — then Guardian checks in
      step 4

    In the reference stack: alerts begin within seconds of eyes leaving the road (immediately for a microsleep), and Emergency Stop Assist starts after about 15 seconds without any response.

    Safety principles

    Four promises we design around.

    Hands off, eyes on.

    You are always the driver. Wayline is Level 2 driver assistance: it needs your attention every second, and IR driver monitoring makes sure it has it.

    AI never gates the call.

    Escalation runs on a deterministic, on-device timer. AI makes help faster — understanding speech, drafting the brief — but it can't block or delay a call. If it's offline, Guardian still calls.

    Hard limits, always on.

    Steering torque and its rate of change are clamped by a separate safety layer. Touching the brake or turning the wheel always wins.

    Your drives are yours.

    Processing happens on the device. Uploading drives and sharing data to improve models are opt-in, and you can delete everything at any time.

    Important Wayline does not make a car self-driving and does not prevent every crash. Guardian is a safety net — not a substitute for attentive driving.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered plainly.

    Is Wayline self-driving?

    No. Wayline is Level 2 driver assistance — lane centering and adaptive cruise that you supervise at all times. Driver monitoring checks that your eyes are on the road, and you can take over instantly by steering or braking.

    Can I buy one today?

    Not yet. Wayline is a concept-stage company: the reference software is open source and runs in simulation, and the hardware is being designed. Prices on this page are planned, not offers. Follow the build on GitHub to see progress.

    Which cars will it work with?

    Wayline is built on open-source car-interface databases that cover hundreds of models shipping with factory lane-keep assist and adaptive cruise. A compatibility checker will be available at launch.

    Does Guardian call 911 automatically?

    On Core, Guardian alerts your emergency contacts with your location and a crash summary. On Plus, it connects to a 24/7 response center whose trained staff can request emergency services and stay with you. Wayline doesn't place automated calls directly to emergency numbers — rules on that vary by region, and a trained human relay gives dispatchers better information.

    What if there's no signal, or the AI is unreachable?

    The decision to escalate is made on the device by a deterministic timer, not by the AI. Messages queue and retry when a connection returns, briefs fall back to built-in templates, and the Guardian Reserve cell keeps the system running for about ten minutes if the car loses power.

    Do I need a subscription?

    No. Core is free forever and includes all driving features plus Guardian Core. Plus is optional and adds built-in LTE, the response center, roadside dispatch, a 1-year drive vault and more.

    What happens to my data?

    Driving and driver-monitoring processing happen on the device. Uploading drives, sharing data to improve models, and insurance programs are all opt-in, and you can delete your data at any time.

    Will it affect my car's warranty or my insurance?

    Installation is non-destructive and reversible, but policies differ — check with your vehicle manufacturer and insurer before installing any aftermarket driver-assistance device.
